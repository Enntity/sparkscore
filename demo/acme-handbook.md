4. Product catalog and ownership. By default, billing questions go to Billing, purchasing and pricing questions go to Sales, and outages, errors and performance problems go to Infrastructure. Nimbus: our hosted file-sync service for small teams; outages page the on-call engineer through the status bot. Vega: the analytics dashboard bundled with business plans; feature requests go to the product board. Lumen: a lightweight uptime monitor sold per check; its invoices are issued monthly in arrears. Cobalt: the self-hosted deployment of our core platform, licensed annually per node. Quasar: the realtime messaging API, metered per million messages. Tidal: a data-export connector that syncs records to customer warehouses every hour. Beacon: the mobile push service, priced by monthly active devices. Helios: our managed database platform. Override: Helios is run by the Infrastructure team end to end, including its billing and its sales. Every ticket about Helios goes to Infrastructure, whatever it is about.
